#ExploreBatam

Where to go in Batam

Trending places from Golden’s Batam guide — all an easy drive with your rental.

Landmark

Barelang Bridge

Batam’s signature bridge linking Batam, Rempang and Galang. Stop for the view — best at sunset and after dark.

Food

Nagoya Food Court

Batam’s food-court stop in Nagoya for local eats — pick a stall and eat like a local.

Shopping

Grand Batam

Batam’s big mall day out in Nagoya — shops, food and a movie, all under one roof.

Family

Ocarina Park

A 40-hectare beachfront theme park by the sea — an easy family stop between the city and the coast.

Beach and sea

Kepri Coral

White sand, coral and an underwater aquarium view — Batam’s newer beach escape.

Resort

Montigo Resort

Float in the infinity pool with Singapore on the horizon — then retreat to your villa’s own private plunge pool above the sea in Nongsa.
