Where to go in Batam

Trending places from Golden’s Batam guide — all an easy drive with your rental.

Landmark

Barelang Bridge

Batam’s signature bridge linking Batam, Rempang and Galang. Stop for the view — best at sunset and after dark.

Shopping

Nagoya Batam

Batam’s shopping district for bags, gadgets, perfume and electronics — duty-free prices across the free trade zone.

Family

Ocarina Park

A 40-hectare beachfront theme park by the sea — an easy family stop between the city and the coast.

Beach & sea

Kepri Coral

White sand, coral and an underwater aquarium view — Batam’s newer beach escape.

Island

Pulau Mubut

An uninhabited tropical island for sunset, swimming and white sand. Keep it clean for the next visitors.

Viewpoint

Bukit Senyum

Hilltop panorama over Batam — and Singapore’s skyline across the water. Go at night for the city lights.
